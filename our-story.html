OUR STORY

Where Coffee,
Comfort, and
Kwentuhan Meet.

Inspired by the warmth of Filipino hospitality and the laid-back charm of Tagaytay cafés, Casa Kape is a place where good coffee, comforting food, and meaningful conversations come together.

WHO WE ARE

A Filipino café inspired by tradition.

Casa Kape was born from a love for Filipino culture, comforting food, and meaningful conversations.

From bold Kapeng Barako and rich Sikwate to traditional kakanin and hearty silog meals, every item on our menu celebrates local heritage while offering a modern café experience.

OUR PHILOSOPHY

Rooted in Filipino hospitality.

At Casa Kape, we believe that great food and coffee bring people together. Every cup we serve and every meal we prepare is made with care, quality ingredients, and genuine appreciation for Filipino traditions.

Whether you're sharing breakfast with family, meeting friends for merienda, or simply enjoying a quiet moment with coffee, we want every visit to feel like home.

SUSTAINABILITY

Brewing a greener future.

Casa Kape is committed to protecting the environment through responsible and sustainable practices.

We minimize the use of single-use plastics and choose eco-friendly alternatives whenever possible. Our goal is to reduce waste by using environmentally responsible packaging, reusable materials, and sustainable serving practices.

We also support local products and ingredients whenever available, helping strengthen local communities while reducing our environmental footprint.
